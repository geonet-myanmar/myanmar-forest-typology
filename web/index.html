Pick a State/Region, District or Township (MIMU PCode v9.4), or the whole country. Search accepts English or Myanmar names. Statistics for these units are precomputed at 10 m and open instantly.

    Draw on the map. Polygons close on the first point (or double-click). Statistics for drawn areas run live in Earth Engine at 10 m (about 3 s per 1,000 km²).

    Layer

    Low values mark pixels where the model hesitates between classes. Scores are relative confidence (ensemble softmax), not calibrated probabilities.

    Confidence threshold

    Off = every pixel takes its highest-scoring class (the published rule). Raising it keeps only pixels the model is sure of: fewer false positives, more misses. The same threshold applies to the Analyze tab.

    Choose an area first (Area tab), or start with the whole country.

    In Myanmar, ForTy maps almost no Planted forest (its score never exceeds 0.01 inside the country) and only about 140 km² of Plantation forest nationwide. Teak and other plantations in Myanmar are therefore counted under other classes. Check any planted area against local data.
